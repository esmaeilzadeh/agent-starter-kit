# Representative returning-developer story

As the developer returning to my repository, I can open the WB-003 delivery task, recover the behavior it promises, and inspect the evidence that supports its recorded result.

The selected source is the historical `structured-agentic-environment` workstream. This pilot model records a present-day inspection route; it does not transfer that workstream’s approval, task completion, execution status, or evidence authority to `revising-workbench`. The source model, TaskResult, reports, logs, specification, plan, and test plan remain read-only.

The model binds the task to the exact `Projections and evidence` specification passage and the `Work breakdown` row for WB-003. The concrete flow remains `WB-evidence-identity`, evaluated by the existing evidence authority. Missing or invalid retained authority blocks the demo; fixture data and excerpts cannot substitute for it.
