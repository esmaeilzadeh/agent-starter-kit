# Follow-up recommendations from the reliability review

R1/R2 are implemented in this workstream. R3–R5 remain unexecuted proposals;
passing this workstream's tests does not validate their expected benefits.

## R3: Independently validate human intent

The companion decision-review-harness-note.md was not found in this checkout.
Do not invent its contents. A later pilot should independently elicit a few
behavioral expectations, trace original requirements to decisions/evidence,
leave unsupported assumptions unconfirmed, and reopen decisions when accepted
requirements change. Preserve defaults-OK and Spec Change authority. Pilot
checks: expose an omitted requirement, retain an unconfirmed assumption, route
an accepted requirement change through Spec Change, and avoid asking again for
already confirmed decisions. Include a whole-system walkthrough.

## R4: Scale artifact depth to task consequence

Keep required artifacts and authority boundaries. Pilot brief complete
artifacts for low-consequence, low-uncertainty, reversible changes; deeper ones
for integrations/migrations/architecture. Record human preparation/review time
and interruptions before changing policy. Line count alone is not a risk or
artifact-depth measure. No process change or pilot was performed here.

## R5: Compare harness and baseline performance

Use comparable tasks and equal acceptance checks with/without the harness.
Record model/runtime, repository revision, task, environment and human help.
Include small corrections, medium features, interrupted work and consequential
integrations. Measure requirement fidelity, defects, human effort, recovery,
provenance and delivery effort. Repeat comparable cases and establish a
baseline before proposing percentage improvements. This workstream supplies
regression evidence for R1/R2 only; no comparative evaluation was run.
