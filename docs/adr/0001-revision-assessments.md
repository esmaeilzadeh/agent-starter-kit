# Assess revision transitions and consumer retention separately

On 2026-10-29, the human selected one transition assessment over separate linked
version assessments, then confirmed the complete interpretation below. Both
submitted responses were `rec`, accepting the displayed recommendations in Q1
and Q2 of the `grill-with-docs` interview.

One revision assessment covers P1 → P2 in a captured project context. Its required
coverage is derived from the actual dependencies of both versions, including
shared obligations. Added, removed, and retained inputs require assessment;
missing required evidence leaves the assessment unresolved. A complete assessment
with applicable authority resolves that transition. Encountering P2 separately
during traversal does not create a requirement for duplicate approval of P2.

Each affected consumer still needs an applicable assessment. T can retain its P1
pin only through explicit reviewed validity for T in the new context, including
resolution of shared input Q and every other relevant input. Historical pins and
executions preserve their original identities. Current completion still requires
the execution evidence applicable to its candidate and contract.

Keeping the transition assessment together avoids split approvals that leave one
side unresolved. Keeping retention scoped to consumers prevents that transition
approval from granting broader permission to use P1 or reuse its evidence.

This records the human's design choice within the accepted behavioral contract;
it leaves the production mechanism open. The normative requirements are in
[reassessment and propagation stopping](../../specs/proposals/shorter-hashes.md#normative-propagation-stop-decision)
and its required decision examples. Terminology is in
[CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md). This decision records no test result, independent
candidate approval, task integration, or workstream acceptance.
